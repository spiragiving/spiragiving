Our story / Denver, Colorado

Giving culture room to grow.

Spira Giving Colorado is a Denver-based private foundation supporting charitable organizations in arts and culture.

Arts open up new perspectives. / Illustrative photography

Local roots. Shared possibility.

For the creative life
of our community.

Colorado’s cultural life is shaped by the people and organizations who create, perform, teach and bring us together.

Our foundation’s giving supports selected charitable organizations. The public record includes music organizations, community radio and arts education in Colorado.

Established in 2022, Spira Giving Colorado received its IRS exemption ruling in August of that year. Public records classify the foundation in arts, culture and humanities.
