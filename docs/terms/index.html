A shared understanding.

The purpose of this website and the limits of its information and inquiry tools.

Updated October 10, 2026

Informational purpose

This website introduces Spira Giving Colorado and summarizes public records and sourced cultural news. It does not offer an open grant program, a financial product or a guarantee of future foundation funding.

Grants and inquiries

The foundation’s public filing states that it only makes contributions to preselected charitable organizations and does not accept unsolicited requests for funds. Contacting the foundation does not constitute a grant application or a funding commitment.

Giving and volunteering

Giving and volunteer forms prepare email drafts only. They do not transfer funds, create recurring payments, issue receipts, submit volunteer applications or confirm placement. Confirm any payment instructions and the intended recipient before making a contribution.

Sources and accuracy

Financial summaries identify fiscal years and link to public filings. Public records may be amended or updated. The complete original filings should be consulted for financial details. This site does not provide individualized tax or legal advice.

Third-party content

Recipient listings identify grants reported in particular fiscal years. They do not imply endorsement, an ongoing relationship or permission to speak on behalf of a recipient. External news summaries reflect the source’s stated publication date.

Responsible use

Use this website lawfully and do not send malicious, abusive or misleading inquiries. Please report factual errors through the contact page so they can be reviewed.
