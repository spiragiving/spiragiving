Website information / Privacy

Your information, handled clearly.

How this website handles contact drafts, technical requests and the photography used in its design.

Updated October 10, 2026

Contact and interest forms

Information entered in a form stays in the current page’s memory. The website does not transmit form responses to a database or store them in browser storage. Navigating away or reloading clears the form. Downloading a draft saves a text file on your device.

Choosing “Open email app” passes the draft to your configured email application. You decide whether to send it. Your email provider’s privacy terms apply, and any message you send is handled through email rather than this website.

Website hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting infrastructure receives technical request data, such as IP address and browser information, when you visit. See GitHub’s privacy statement for hosting-related information.

Analytics and cookies

This website does not add advertising trackers, analytics tools or form-storage cookies. Images and fonts are served with the site. External websites and email services have their own policies.

External links

Public filings and cultural organization links open third-party websites. Their information handling and content are separate from this website.

Illustrative photography

The arts photographs are design illustrations and do not depict Spira Giving Colorado programs, officers, grant recipients or a foundation event.

Questions and corrections

Contact contact@spiragiving.dev for website inquiries or to flag an error. Please avoid including sensitive financial or identity information in a form or email.
